Demolition Men – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Demolition Men is a game made by OG Studio (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the game and the services it relies on process, why, and what choices you have. Questions about it: OGpriv@proton.me.

In short

  • There are no accounts, and we never ask for your real name, e-mail address or other contact details.
  • Your progress is saved on your device only.
  • Only if you play ranked, our ranked server stores the nickname you chose, an ID made from your device's identifier (not the identifier itself), and your ranked runs and scores. Everyone can see your nickname and scores on the boards. After 180 days they are deleted.
  • The Android version shows ads from Google AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you decide in Google’s consent form whether ads may be personalised, and you can change that at any time under Settings ▸ PRIVACY.
  • Online co-op runs through our relay server. It sees your IP address while you play but does not store it.
  • We don’t sell data. Ranked play is the only part of the game that sends us data about you.

1. Who is responsible

For the game, the relay server and the ranked server, the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is OG Studio, reachable at OGpriv@proton.me. For advertising, Google is responsible for its own processing (section 5).

2. Data stored on your device

The game keeps a save file on your device with your progress, best scores, in-game cash and upgrades, your settings, and counters that space out ads (for example, how many runs since the last ad). It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

3. Online co-op

Online play is optional; the whole game can be played offline. When you host or join an online game, your device connects to our relay server over an encrypted connection (TLS). The relay pairs two players by a join code and passes the game data between them (positions, hits, damage to buildings) without reading or storing it. Players never see each other’s IP address.

To do this, the relay processes:

  • Your IP address, needed to send data to your device. It is kept in memory while you are connected and for up to one minute afterwards, to stop abuse (too many connections or wrong join codes from one address). It is never written to logs or to disk.
  • The join code, which exists only while the game room is open.
  • Your nickname and player ID (section 4), which the joining device sends to the host with the join, so a ranked co-op run carries both players' names. The relay passes them on without reading or storing them.

The relay records only totals (rooms open, players connected, traffic, why connections ended), without addresses or codes. It runs in a data centre of Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany, which hosts it for us.

Legal basis: providing the online play you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in protecting the service against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Ranked boards

Ranked play is optional. RANKED in the game has one level a day for everyone, with a board for solo and one for co-op. When you play it, your device talks to our ranked server over an encrypted connection (TLS), and the server processes:

  • Your nickname: the name you type on the game's first screen, or the one the game makes up (like WRECKER 4821) until you do. It is shown on the boards next to your scores, to everyone who opens them. Please don't use your real name.
  • A player ID: a one-way code (a SHA-256 hash) of your device's own identifier, or, in a browser, of a random number the game keeps in your browser's storage. It gives one device one nickname and one place on each board. The identifier itself never leaves your device, and we can't work it back from the code.
  • Your ranked runs: a record of each run (where your crew was every tenth of a second, what was hit with what, pickups, actions, the time and the score) and our server's check of it. The server replays it with the game's rules to keep the boards free of cheats. The best run of each day is also shown to other players as a "ghost" they can race.
  • In online co-op, the host's device sends the run with the nickname and player ID of both players.
  • Reports: when you report a run as a cheat, which run you reported.
  • Your IP address, to send the answers to your device and to limit how often one address may start runs, make new players or report. It is kept in memory only, at most a day, and never written to logs or to disk.

We look at runs the server turns down and runs that are reported. A player whose runs don't hold up may be hidden from the boards; they still see their own runs. The server's logs note when a run is turned down or reported, with the player ID; they are rotated and kept at most 30 MB.

Legal basis: providing the ranked play you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in fair boards without cheats (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Advertising (Android version)

The Android version shows a full-screen ad between some runs and, only if you choose to watch one, a rewarded ad that doubles a run’s cash. The ads come from Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and Switzerland, and by Google LLC elsewhere (including the UK). The PC and browser versions show no ads.

According to Google, its Mobile Ads SDK collects:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • interactions with the app and its ads, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the SDK.

Google uses this to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud, for analytics, and, only if you allow it where consent is required, to personalise ads.

Your choice. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form appears when you first start the game, and no ads are requested until you have made a choice. If you don’t consent, Google shows non-personalised or limited ads, which use less data. You can change your choice at any time under Settings ▸ PRIVACY. Wherever you live, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (under Privacy ▸ Ads, or Google ▸ Ads, depending on the device).

We only receive aggregated reports from AdMob, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned, never data about you. How Google uses data from apps that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Legal basis: your consent for personalised ads and for storing or reading information on your device where the law requires it (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); otherwise our legitimate interest in funding a free game through ads (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Google Play

If you install the game from Google Play, Google processes data about the download and your device under its own privacy policy. Google Play gives us aggregated statistics (installs, ratings) and crash and performance reports (such as the device model, the Android version and the error) that don’t identify you.

7. Other services

  • The analytics, crash-reporting and performance-reporting services of the game engine (Unity) are switched off.
  • If you play the browser version on itch.io, itch.io’s privacy policy covers its website.
  • This page is served without access logs.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal data from them. Ranked nicknames are public: the game turns down offensive ones, and others can be reported.

9. How long data is kept

  • Save file: on your device until you delete it.
  • Relay: IP addresses in memory while you are connected and up to one minute afterwards; game data is not stored.
  • Ranked server: nicknames, player IDs, runs and reports for 180 days, then deleted automatically; its daily backups are kept for 7 days more. IP addresses only in memory, at most a day.
  • Advertising data: as described in Google’s policies.

10. Transfers outside the EU

The relay and the ranked server run in the EU. Google may process data in other countries, including the United States, under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time (Settings ▸ PRIVACY) without affecting processing before that. Our ranked server checks runs automatically; that only decides what shows on a board, nothing with legal or similar effects, and if you ask, a person looks at it again.

If you never played ranked, we don’t store data about you; your save file is under your own control on your device. If you did, write to us with your nickname (and roughly when you played) to see, correct or delete your ranked data. For data Google holds, use Google’s controls described above. You can also complain to the data protection authority of the country where you live or work.

If you live in a US state with a privacy law, you can limit ad personalisation with the advertising ID controls in your Android settings.

12. Security

Connections to the relay, the ranked server and Google’s ad services are encrypted (TLS). The relay doesn’t store game data or addresses. The ranked server keeps only what section 4 lists, on our server in the EU; the game has no accounts or passwords that could leak.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Significant changes are also mentioned in the game’s update notes.
